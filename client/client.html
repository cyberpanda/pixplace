    About Pixplace
    Pixplace

    A modern 2D avatar chat for the browser, in the spirit of The Palace. Click in the room to walk your avatar there. Clicking a name opens the context menu – there you find "Whisper" and further actions. An overview of all available commands is available with ::help.

    Home: chat.pixplace.org · github.com/cyberpanda/pixplace
